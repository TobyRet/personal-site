## The situation

The fund's member portal team needed front-end cover. I joined for one month.

The fund extended the contract each month, to five months in total. For part of that time I also covered the Innovation Manager role.

## The constraints

- The fund is APRA-regulated, so changes had to meet strict quality and release controls.
- Releases were manual and usually happened once a month.

## What I did

- I released two member journeys, for withdrawals and pension sign-up, from design to production.
- I built the first automated deployment pipeline for the platform. It replaces the manual monthly release.
- I improved code quality. I added Prettier and Stylelint and upgraded the build tools. I found the Sass changes needed before Dart Sass 3.0, and wrote technical spikes.
- I helped hire a permanent front-end engineer and onboarded them in person.

## The result

- Two member journeys went live.
- The platform ran with no unplanned outages during the cover period.
- The team has an automated deployment pipeline and a permanent front-end engineer.

## Stack

React and TypeScript, with Sass for styles. Figma for design handover. Azure DevOps for code, pipelines and work tracking.
