## The situation

The client runs a global student housing platform with 3 million residents in 25 countries. It had bought an unstable legacy rental platform. The platform had no automated tests and no monitoring.

I led an Equal Experts team in Australia and India. The work started as modernisation. When the client's priorities changed, it moved to stability and team improvement.

## The constraints

- The legacy core had no tests, so any change to it was a risk.
- US rental fee transparency rules had a fixed deadline, with regulatory penalties if it was missed.
- The team was spread across several countries and time zones.

## What I did

- I delivered the US rental fee transparency changes quickly. We built new modules in isolation with a controlled switchover, so the legacy core did not change.
- I added the first monitoring to the platform, with Datadog and Sentry.
- I introduced automated tests, pull request reviews and code standards for the offshore developers in India and Pakistan.
- I led technical discovery and proposed a modernisation plan based on the strangler pattern.

## The result

- The client avoided regulatory penalties.
- The team could see production errors for the first time, and had data to support modernisation.
- The team moved from firefighting to planned work.
- The client has a modernisation plan it can use for future work.

## Stack

PHP Laravel for the legacy platform. TypeScript and NestJS for the new modules. Datadog and Sentry for monitoring.
