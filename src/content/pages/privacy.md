## The contact form

When you send a message, the site collects your name, email address, company (if you give one) and your message. I use these only to reply to you. The message is sent to me by email. The site does not store it.

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that you are a person. Turnstile does not use cookies for tracking.

## Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites.

## Your rights

You can ask me what data I hold about you, and ask me to delete it. Use the contact form or LinkedIn.
