I am a technical lead and senior full-stack engineer. I help organisations build new products, steady legacy platforms and meet fixed deadlines, often in regulated environments. I contract through my company, Byte Size Australia.

## Before engineering

I spent ten years in marketing, communications and digital product before I became an engineer. After teaching in Japan on the JET Programme, I joined <a href="https://www.youthmusic.org.uk/" rel="external">Youth Music</a>, a national UK charity, in 2007. I started as Marketing and Web Manager, then ran a national workforce programme with nine partner organisations. I led the build and launch of the Youth Music Network, an online community for music educators. As Communications Manager, I led a team of four across PR, content, design and IT, with my own budget.

In these roles I decided what we built, worked with designers and developers to deliver it, and was responsible for the result. I still bring that product view to my engineering work.

## Becoming an engineer

I retrained as a software engineer at Makers in 2014. I then spent three years as a consultant at Codurance, a software craftsmanship consultancy. I worked with clients such as Amazon Web Services and Mergermarket, and helped teams adopt Extreme Programming practices.

## UK public sector and contract work

In London I worked on public services. I helped rebuild data.gov.uk at the Government Digital Service. I built services for the Ministry of Justice and the Department for Education, including Find Postgraduate Teacher Training and Apply for Teacher Training. I also mentored junior developers and helped recruit engineers.

I then contracted for commercial clients. I helped rebuild Abcam’s global e-commerce platform, which launched in the US, Europe, China and Japan, and I worked on Mastercard’s new fraud detection tools.

## Work in Australia

I moved my family to Australia in 2025. Since then I have:

- led the front-end build of a schedule-checking system for a major Australian airline, through Terem
- led an Equal Experts team in Australia and India to [steady a global student housing platform](/work/student-housing-platform-stabilisation/)
- covered two roles at an APRA-regulated super fund, and helped build its first automated deployment pipeline
- [taken Amador](/work/amador/), a creator marketplace, from an inherited prototype to a live product as its lead engineer
